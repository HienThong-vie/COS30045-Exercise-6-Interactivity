Exercise 6: Explore TV energy use

A histogram with filters (6.1, 6.2) and a scatterplot with a legend and tooltips (6.3, 6.4), built from 4,233 TV models in the January 2026 Energy Rating data.

6.1 & 6.2 Three in four TVs use under 600 kWh a year

Filter by screen type and size to see how the distribution of energy use changes. Hover a bar to see its range and count.

Screen type
Screen size

Data: Ex6_TVdata_withStar.csv – Energy Rating TV registrations (Jan 2026). One model above 2,600 kWh stretches the x axis.

6.3 & 6.4 More stars, less energy

Each dot is a TV model. Colour shows the screen type; hover a dot to see the brand, model and screen size. The filters above also fade the dots that do not match.

Star rating comes from the star column of the data set.
